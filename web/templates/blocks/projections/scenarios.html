Wealth paths

Each simulated future replays your real history: every month picks one of your past months at random, grows the balance by it, and adds your average deposit. The solid typical path is the middle outcome of 500 such simulations; the shaded band is where 80% of them landed - lucky above, unlucky below. The thin dashed average month, every month line grows at exactly your historical average rate instead - no lucky or unlucky sequences - so it is perfectly smooth, and the typical path usually ends slightly below it (volatility drag).
